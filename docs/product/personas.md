# User Personas 👤

## Persona 1: Alex — The Solo Backpacker
- **Age**: 26
- **Occupation**: Environmental Researcher
- **Goal**: Explore off-the-beaten-path destinations on a budget with reliable offline maps and weather updates.
- **Pain Point**: Reaching remote areas with no cellular signal and losing access to saved itineraries or offline map tiles.
- **OpenTrail Value**: Downloadable offline map regions, local SQLite trip caching, and Open-Meteo weather insights.

---

## Persona 2: Sarah & Family — The Detailed Vacation Planners
- **Age**: 38
- **Occupation**: Product Manager
- **Goal**: Organize a 10-day family trip to Japan with structured day-by-day itineraries, attraction details, and smart packing lists.
- **Pain Point**: Managing scattered notes across multiple paid travel apps with intrusive advertisements.
- **OpenTrail Value**: Drag-and-drop daily itinerary planner, smart weather-driven packing checklists, and localized UI in Japanese and English.

---

## Persona 3: Omar — The Remote Digital Nomad
- **Age**: 32
- **Occupation**: Software Engineer
- **Goal**: Track visited countries, explore local cultural hubs, and plan multi-city workcations across the Middle East and Europe.
- **Pain Point**: Lack of native RTL support and privacy concerns over location tracking in commercial apps.
- **OpenTrail Value**: Native Arabic/Urdu RTL layout support, zero cloud data tracking, and milestone statistics tracking.
